## 3. Stimulus and split audit

**Pass.** `40_stimuli.py` derives the lexicon from UD-EWT train tokens only, applies the stated
surface/frequency rules, assigns deterministic lemma-disjoint pilot/dev/test splits, holds
attractors out of dev, validates one-token verb forms, aligns the last overlapping subject
subword, and constructs opposite-number donors from the exact matched pair. Same-number donor
pairing is deterministic and avoids self-donation for groups of size at least two.

The artifacts distinguish the eligible lexicon pool (460 lemmas; 422 transparent, 38
nontransparent) from instantiated study splits (5 pilot + 200 dev + 90 test = 295 lemmas).
Some public prose calls 460 the noun lexicon, which is correct, but it must not call 460 the
study/test stimulus sample.

C3 eligibility is correctly based on equal singular/plural subject token counts within each
matched pair. The C3 population is therefore a subset of the primary population.

## 4. Behavioural readout

**Pass.** The authoritative baseline and intervention paths load the pinned checkpoint in
fp32 and read `model(...).logits` from the trained untied `embed_out` head at the stored final
stem position. No stale manual tied-head readout remains in these paths. The checkpoint's
`tie_word_embeddings: false` choice and the pilot readout correction are documented in ADR
006. The reported large logit scale explains the fp16 quantization concern but does not alter
the contrastive interpretation after consistent fp32 execution.

## 5. Probe-direction reconstruction

**Pass.** The code implements `w_x = w_z / scale`, normalizes it, and aligns the sign so
positive projection is plural. The transformed-intercept equivalence is unit-tested; training
uses the train split, fixed `C=0.01`, cap, and seeds; and the chosen C is checked against the
reproduced v1.0 artifact. The direction manifest shows exact refit/v1.0 balanced-accuracy
agreement at every point, including embedding 0.9825686 and block_11 0.9772055.

The fp16 v1.0 activation extraction versus fp32 behavioural pass is an acknowledged precision
boundary, not a material direction-reconstruction error: the direction refit reproduces the
v1.0 predictions exactly.

## 6. Intervention hooks

**Pass, based on code and artifacts.** The hook changes only `t[0, subject_last]`, and the
direction patch preserves the orthogonal residual component by construction. Embedding,
GPT-NeoX blocks, and `final_layer_norm` are all mapped explicitly; hooks are removed in a
`finally` block; logits are read only at `final_token_pos`; and cached residuals use the same
subject position later patched.

C0 reports zero maximum logit discrepancy in pilot/dev/test. The terminal diagnostic is the
expected architectural sanity check: block_11 and ln_f are exactly zero for near/attractor
templates and nonzero only for simple templates, where the subject is the prediction position.
The pure hook semantics remain only partially covered by automated tests because the actual
model-dependent forward-hook path is not unit-tested with a live GPT-NeoX module.

## 7. Competence gate

**Pass.** On dev, Gate 1 is 7.1967 with CI [7.0597, 7.3275], and Gate 2 is 1.0 with CI
[1.0, 1.0]. The paired subject-number construction and lemma-cluster bootstrap match the
pre-registered definition.

## 8. Primary H2 statistical verdict

**Pass.** The primary effect is the stated per-layer mean donor-directed shift, with lemma
cluster bootstrap CIs. All 13 causal-point CIs exclude zero: embedding 4.839 [4.638, 5.026]
through block_11 0.119 [0.115, 0.123]. This supports the bounded claim that the tested
one-dimensional intervention changes verb-number preference toward the donor.

## 9. H3 specificity/control verdict

**Repair required.** The implementation computes primary and control CIs separately versus
zero, then generates a conclusion saying the primary effect is relative to same-number and
random controls. It does not compute the pre-registered paired primary-minus-control
contrast CIs.

Because the same item/verb rows are available under every condition, the missing analysis
should use paired per-item contrasts, then lemma-cluster bootstrap those contrasts. I
independently recomputed this from the committed test outputs. Examples:

- embedding primary-minus-same: 4.8545, CI [4.6429, 5.0523]
- embedding primary-minus-random-mean: 4.8063, CI [4.6072, 4.9890]
- block_11 primary-minus-same: 0.1190, CI [0.1130, 0.1245]
- block_11 primary-minus-random-mean: 0.1193, CI [0.1147, 0.1234]

These support the intended specificity result, but the implementation and snapshot must record
the paired contrasts before H3 is presented as formally tested.

## 10. Full-residual control

**Pass with population caveat.** C3 is positive at every layer and is correctly restricted to
the matched-tokenization subset (`n_items=6960` per layer versus 7200 for primary). The report
correctly identifies it as a broader positive control, but comparisons must retain the subset
qualification.

The published “roughly 60%” statement is not supported by the displayed headline values:
4.839 / 6.424 is about 75%, not 60%. Since the populations differ, the safest repair is to
remove the ratio; alternatively report a ratio computed from a subset-matched primary estimate.

## 11. Bootstrap and inference

**Primary bootstrap pass; H3 contrast omission noted above.** `cluster_mean_bootstrap` resamples
unique lemmas with replacement and moves all rows for each drawn lemma together. It returns
the full-sample mean and percentile CI. The gate's `(lemma, verb)` rows are formed after
averaging templates, as pre-registered. Repeated verb/template/layer rows are not treated as
independent clusters.

The separate per-layer CIs are descriptive/pre-registered estimands; the report should not
turn “all layers exclude zero” into an uncorrected claim of a familywise layerwise discovery.
No automatic multiplicity correction is required for the stated descriptive curve.

## 12. Causal interpretation

**Pass with bounded wording.** The evidence supports causal contribution of the decoded
direction under this intervention. It does not establish necessity, uniqueness, mediation, a
grammar circuit, or grammatical understanding. “Decoded noun-number direction” is preferable
to “abstract grammatical-number representation,” especially at shallow layers where
orthographic/surface information is a live confound. Scalar donor projection replacement can
still transfer some lexical/surface-correlated information; the existing matched donors and
controls do not eliminate that possibility.

## 13. Decodability versus causal effect

**Pass after scope correction.** The layerwise profiles differ strongly: selectivity rises
from about 0.218 to 0.353 while the causal effect decays from about 4.84 to 0.12. The report
mostly frames this correctly as a difference between these metrics. It should say the
layerwise profiles differ under the tested metrics, not imply a general inverse relation or
that selectivity measures mechanistic “use.” The 13 layer points are not treated as independent
replicates.

## 14. Provenance and reproducibility

## 16. Gatekeeper Concerns A–E

- **A — H3 paired contrasts:** confirmed. The current code is missing the direct paired
  primary-minus-same and primary-minus-random CIs. Add them from existing outputs; no GPU run.
- **B — random controls at zero:** confirmed as an overstatement. Several random CIs exclude
  zero (embedding 0.0328 [0.0260, 0.0395], block_5 0.0663 [0.0608, 0.0719], block_11 a small
  negative CI). Describe them as much smaller than primary, not identically zero.
- **C — roughly 60%:** confirmed numerically unsupported. Headline ratio is about 75%, and C3
  is a different subset. Remove or recompute on a common subset.
- **D — 460 versus 295:** confirmed as a terminology risk. 460 is the eligible lexicon pool;
  295 lemmas are instantiated across pilot/dev/test, with 90 confirmatory test lemmas. Clarify
  public prose wherever “study/stimulus lemmas” is used.
- **E — pooled subgroup means:** confirmed as a labeling limitation, not a core defect. The
  subgroup summaries average across causal points and must be labeled that way; a layer-by-
  subgroup table/figure would be preferable if the subgroup interpretation is retained.
